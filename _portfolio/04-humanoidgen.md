**TeleAI, Shanghai | 03.2025 - 06.2025**

- Proposed action frame annotations to guide effective motion generation for bimanual dexterous manipulation, and developed a SAPIEN-based simulation framework leveraging these annotations to synthesize high-quality demonstrations.
- Leveraged LLMs to generate task files and applied MCTS for optimization to improve task validity in generation.
- Collected expert demonstrations to train Diffusion Policy and 3D Diffusion Policy (DP3), and benchmarked their performance across both proposed simulations and real-world tasks.
